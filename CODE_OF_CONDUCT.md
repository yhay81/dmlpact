# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically honest project.

Be considerate, welcome questions, critique ideas rather than people, disclose
uncertainty, and prioritize user safety over winning an argument. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, and sexualized or
degrading conduct are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method
on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in context and may result in a
warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion.
Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers,
review spaces, and public settings where someone represents the project.
