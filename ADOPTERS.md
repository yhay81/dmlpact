# Adopters

No public adopters have been reported yet.

If you evaluate DMLPact, open a Discussion or pull request with information you
are comfortable sharing:

- organization or project name;
- PostgreSQL version and deployment shape;
- DML workflow and approximate scale;
- safety refusal or near miss that proved useful;
- the mutation, rollback, or escalation decision DMLPact improved;
- whether use repeated after at least 30 days;
- false refusals, missed risk, limit escapes, or unreceipted mutations;
- missing capability or operational concern;
- a sanitized evidence link and permission to list the report when available.

Anonymous and private feedback is also valuable through the maintainer's GitHub
contact. Do not share credentials, production SQL, or customer data.
